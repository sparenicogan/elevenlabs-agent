# Helvetia Werkstoffe AG — general information

True for every customer. Nothing here depends on who is calling, so none of it needs the
caller verified.

## Payment

Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. An invoice is overdue from the day after.

Invoices are emailed on the day of issue. Payment is by bank transfer, quoting the payment
reference printed on the invoice.

Everything is in Swiss francs. We answer in German, French, Italian and English.

## Sites

- **Fribourg** — the factory, and the office attached to it.
- **Zug** — the head office, where billing sits.
- **Ticino** — the sales agency.

Open Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00.
